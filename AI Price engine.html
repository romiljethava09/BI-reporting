2 · Company floor / minimum rates

Two editable base rates per ACRISS group — €/day for days 1–10, and €/day from day 11 onward. Every other column (1 Tag … 30 Tage) is the total price for that rental length, calculated automatically as days × the applicable daily rate. This is Wheego's price floor — the calculator never undercuts below it when "enforce floor" is on.

%
When on: this combines with your undercut/category rule above rather than replacing it. The #1 competitor's price is first adjusted up or down by the star-rating gap — for every 0.1★ Wheego is rated higher, the reference price goes up by this percentage; for every 0.1★ lower, it goes down by the same percentage (0.1★ → 4%, 0.2★ → 8%, 0.3★ → 12%, and so on up to a 10★ gap, by default) — and then your flat/percentage undercut (or a matching category rule) is applied on top of that adjusted price, e.g. "rating +0.3★ then −2 €". Floor rate still applies. "Keep if already #1" still wins outright if it applies. Needs a "Rating" column and Wheego's own listing in the import.

Category price rules override the default undercut per ACRISS group, optionally per date

Set a different rule for one or several groups at once, and one or several dates at once — e.g. MBMN + ECAR always 2 € cheaper than the cheapest competitor, PVMR always 10 € more expensive, or a group 5 € more expensive only on two specific dates. Click the Group/Date boxes to open a checklist. A rule scoped to specific dates wins over an "All dates" rule for the same group; groups with no rule keep using the default undercut above.

6 · Final export

XLSX matches your Wheelsys_Rate.xlsx template exactly — same 18 columns, same order (the table below). CSV matches your FRA_AP legacy 27-column format instead (Group, Rate Name, Rate code, Booking/Pickup dates, Priority, Company, the 10 rate columns, Weekend, Rate zone, Location flags, WG level, Additionals — with the same 4-row header). Both generate one row per ACRISS group for every pickup date found in your import (9 dates in, 9 rows per group out, etc). All rate columns are per-day rates (total price ÷ rental length), not totals — falling back to your floor table's daily rate when no competitor data exists for that length.
